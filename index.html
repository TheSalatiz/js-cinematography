About me

Hi, I'm Juan.

I grew up in Colombia, where I had a dream bigger than myself, but it really took shape after moving to the United States to study multimedia and film. Leaving home meant learning to observe the world closely, figuring out how to translate emotion, environment, and human connection into a compelling narrative.

That path eventually led me to Las Vegas, a city that operates at a hundred miles an hour, filled with noise, neon, and massive celebrations. When the room is loud and the energy is frantic, I'm focused on telling the story. I'm naturally drawn to the unscripted, deeply human moments that happen when people stop performing and just exist together.
