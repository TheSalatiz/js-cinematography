The half-day shoot, scheduled around your patients, and about 10 minutes a week to approve videos.

Yes. Three times a week from your own account, after you approve each video. I can add TikTok and Facebook too.

No, and nobody honest can. What I promise is the work, on schedule, and a report that shows exactly what happened.
